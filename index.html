Our story

Welcome to Solaris.

I've been around horses for about twenty years. I dreamed of them since I was a little girl, but only made that dream come true as an adult. In 2022 we moved to Moldova and brought our whole family with us — our dog Kora and my horse Francesca.

Horses are herd animals — they can't live alone. So to keep Francesca company we welcomed a pony, Malysh (Little One), and later an Orlov Trotter, Dobry (Kind One), joined us too.

My goal is simple: to give children and adults the chance to connect with these amazing animals. Time with horses is a proven way to lift your mood and improve both mental and physical health. Come and discover their world with us.

“We're not a competitive club — our mission is relaxed, happy family time.”

Experiences

Ways to spend time with the horses.

Everything is calm, supervised and matched to you — whether it's your first time in the saddle or you've ridden for years.

01

Horseback riding

Guided rides in the saddle, always supervised and matched to each rider's age and comfort.

02

Pony rides

A gentle introduction for younger and first-time riders, led at a calm, easy pace.

03

Photo sessions

Thirty minutes or more with a horse or pony, and warm keepsake photos to take home.

04

Field trips

Ride out into the countryside and open fields around the village, at your own pace.
